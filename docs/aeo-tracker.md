# OCEBOOTS – search and AI answer tracker

How OCEBOOTS shows up when shoppers ask Google and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity,
Google AI Overviews) the questions they ask before buying football boots. Re-test the same
questions every month, note who appears and what they cite, and close the gaps.

## 1. Buyer questions to test

Ask each one in Google and in at least two AI assistants. Record: does OCEBOOTS appear (Y/N), who
appears instead, and which sources are cited.

**Where to buy**
- where to buy football boots in New Zealand
- where to buy pre-owned / used football boots NZ
- best place to buy rare football boots
- where to buy pro-issue football boots
- where can I buy old Nike Mercurials (e.g. Vapor 8, Superfly 4)
- football boot shop Auckland
- is OCEBOOTS legit / OCEBOOTS reviews

**Choosing a boot**
- Mercurial vs Phantom – which is better for me
- Mercurial Vapor vs Superfly
- best football boots for a winger / striker / defender
- Tiempo vs Copa
- what does pro-issue / player edition mean
- SG-Pro vs FG – which should I buy
- is it worth buying second-hand football boots

**Sizing**
- Nike vs adidas football boot sizing
- what is EU 42 2/3 in UK
- do Mercurials run small

**Specific boots (long tail – these are the easiest wins)**
- [model] [size] NZ, e.g. "Mercurial Vapor 14 Elite UK 8 NZ"
- [model] [nickname], e.g. "Superfly 6 Golden Touch", "Vapor 8 CR7 Love Hate"

## 4. Outside the site (EARN / show up where buyers talk)

AI assistants trust what independent sources say about you more than what you say about yourself.

- **Google Search Console**: verify oceboots.com, submit /sitemap.xml, request indexing of the
  guides and main collections.
- **Google Business Profile**: OCEBOOTS, football boot store, Auckland, linking to the site.
- **Reviews**: install a reviews app (e.g. Judge.me) and ask every buyer for a review; also
  collect reviews on Google Business Profile. Trustpilot reviews are what AI cites for the UK
  competitors.
- **Reddit and forums**: r/bootroom and r/soccer cleat threads, NZ football groups. Answer real
  questions helpfully as OCEBOOTS (sizing, pro-issue, older generations), linking a guide only
  where it genuinely answers the question. No promotion-only posts.
- **The players and clubs you have supplied**: ask for a mention or link (club sponsor pages,
  player posts tagging @oceboots).
- **Boot media**: pitch rare pairs or your pro work to boot blogs and YouTube channels
  (footballboots.co.uk lists resellers – ask to be listed).
- **Instagram**: oceboots.com in the bio, and in captions for drops.

## 5. Monthly check

1. Re-run every question in section 1; record OCEBOOTS Y/N, competitors, cited sources.
2. Search Console: impressions and clicks for the guide pages and collections.
3. For each miss: is there a page that answers it? If yes, improve that page; if no, write one;
   if the answer exists but OCEBOOTS still isn't cited, work on outside sources for that topic.
4. Search Console → Search results, and the Generative AI report (AI Overviews / AI Mode
   impressions) once it shows data. Clicks down but AI impressions steady means you are still in
   the AI answers: check which pages lost rankings and which lost click-through.
5. If a competitor replaced OCEBOOTS for a question, read the page that got cited: what did it
   answer that ours skipped (a current model, a size detail, a price, a clear yes/no)? Fix that.
6. After a Google core update, wait a full week after the rollout ends, then compare with the week
   before it started.
